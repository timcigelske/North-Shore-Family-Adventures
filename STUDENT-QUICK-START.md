### Example Analysis:

**Generated Poem:**
```
The Stethoscope — though honored — worn —
May not — the Soul — adorn —
For Art — demands — its own — Physician —
To heal — what's truly — torn —
```

**Your Analysis:**
"The AI generated a poem that speaks directly to my classmate's struggle between medicine and art. The opening line 'The Stethoscope — though honored — worn —' acknowledges the prestige of the medical profession (the 'honored' stethoscope) while suggesting it may not fit her soul. The em dashes create pauses that make us really think about each word. What's brilliant is how the poem flips the medical metaphor - it suggests that ART is the real 'Physician' that can 'heal what's truly torn.' This connects to Dr. Nettleton's observation about Wonder Woman finding her authentic power - just as Diana stepped into her true role, the poem encourages my classmate to recognize that her artistic calling is what will truly heal her sense of living someone else's life. Like Dickinson wrote, 'Tell the truth, but tell it slant' - the poem doesn't directly say 'quit pre-med,' but uses medical imagery to make its point more powerfully."

## 🎨 Understanding Dickinson's Style

The AI captures Emily Dickinson's unique poetic techniques:

### Em Dashes (—)
- Create dramatic pauses
- Make you slow down and think
- Add weight to important words

**Example:** "The Soul — selects — her own Society —"

### Capitalization
- Important concepts are Capitalized
- Draws attention to key ideas
- Creates emphasis on themes

**Example:** Truth, Soul, Path, Choice, Freedom

### Slant Rhyme
- Imperfect rhymes that feel more natural
- More thought-provoking than perfect rhymes
- Characteristic of Dickinson

**Example:** "soul/all" or "mind/find"

### Compressed Language
- Short, powerful lines (6-8 syllables)
- Every word matters
- Dense with meaning

### Nature Metaphors
- Birds, flowers, seasons, natural phenomena
- Used to explain human experiences
- Makes abstract ideas concrete

## ⚠️ Troubleshooting

### "Error: API request failed"
- Check your internet connection (you need to be online)
- Try refreshing the page and generating again
- If it persists, contact your instructor

### File Won't Open
- Try right-clicking → "Open with" → Choose your browser
- Make sure the file ends with `.html` (not `.txt`)
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

### Poem Doesn't Seem Personalized
- Make your reflection more specific
- Add more details about your situation
- Include concrete examples
- Try rephrasing your reflection

### Generation Takes Forever
- Normal generation time: 5-10 seconds
- First time may take longer
- If it's been 30+ seconds, refresh and try again
