# Collection expansion

The current publication has 50 researched campgrounds (42 public, 8 private), with 48 sourced approximate map locations. See [expanded collection notes](expanded-collection.md). The earlier launch records below describe prior versions.

# Source coverage and editorial limits

23 curated Wisconsin campground profiles cover southeastern Wisconsin/Kettle Moraine, Door County, the Lake Michigan shore, Dells/Baraboo and selected central/southwestern Wisconsin parks. All have real operator sources, a meaningful review date, licensed imagery and documented location coordinates. Some landscape images deliberately represent the region rather than an individual campsite and are captioned accordingly.

The initial records answer accommodation, fit, tradeoffs, reservation route, equipment needs and a weekend framework. Public operator coverage remains reader-first, with ordinary official booking links. Not every page has a separately verified indoor attraction; some retain an explicit planning task rather than invent a nearby venue. Volatile fees, current site inventory, measured privacy/noise, exact pad dimensions and tested Wi-Fi remain unknown. No personal stay or original product test is claimed.

Pinewoods electricity is conflicting in the official source and is null pending direct verification. Northern Unit construction notices should be rechecked for plans beginning fall 2027. No new stadium camping, glamping city pages or gear rankings have been published without substantive reporting.
