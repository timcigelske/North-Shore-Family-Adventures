How to Get In Free to Summerfest Milwaukee

Published: January 6, 2026 | Category: Festival Guides

Summerfest is one of Milwaukee's most iconic events, attracting music lovers from all over. But did you know there are several ways to get in for free? Here's your complete guide to enjoying Summerfest without breaking the bank!

1. Opening Day Free Admission

Summerfest traditionally offers free admission on opening day before a certain time. This is one of the easiest ways to enjoy the festival without spending a dime on admission.

2. Partner Promotions

Many local businesses and radio stations run promotions giving away free Summerfest tickets. Keep an eye on social media and local radio stations in May and June.

3. Volunteer Opportunities

Summerfest offers volunteer positions that come with free admission passes. It's a great way to be part of the festival while enjoying the music.

4. Student Discounts

Students can often get discounted or free admission on certain days. Check the official Summerfest website for student-specific promotions.

Conclusion

With these tips, you can enjoy all that Summerfest has to offer without spending money on admission. Don't forget to budget for food, drinks, and any merchandise you might want to purchase!
